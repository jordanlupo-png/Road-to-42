Your data in Road to 42

Road to 42 stores your Google account identifier, email, runner name, selected animal, training goals and activity entries in Supabase. Optional wellbeing notes are private to your account.

Google Health

Connecting Google Health is optional and separate from signing in. With your permission, we read exercise sessions to import running dates, distances, durations and pace from 14 September 2026. We do not import cycling or walking. We do not store GPS routes, heart rate or sleep data from Google Health. Access tokens are stored encrypted on the server.

Community visibility

If you enable leaderboard sharing in Runner settings, signed-in players can see your runner name, animal and training progress, including activity distances, dates and durations. Your email, wellbeing notes and Google Health credentials are not shared with players. You can turn sharing off in Runner settings.

Phone notifications

Phone alerts are optional. If you enable them, Road to 42 stores a browser-generated push subscription so your device can receive XP, badge and level-up alerts even while the game is closed. The subscription contains a device endpoint and encryption keys, not your phone number. You can disable alerts from the notification panel or your phone settings.

Disconnect or delete

Disconnecting Google Health removes the connection and stops new imports. Existing training entries remain until you delete them. Runner → Delete account and data permanently removes your Road to 42 account, its training data, push subscriptions and saved health credentials. Trial accounts can also be deleted during setup. This does not delete your Google or Fitbit account.

The app uses Google for sign-in and health authorization, Supabase for account and data storage, browser push services for optional phone alerts, and GitHub Pages for hosting. Google Health information is used for your running progress and optional community features; it is not used for advertising or sold.
