Using Road to 42

Road to 42 is a game for recording training and celebrating progress. Its levels, rewards and Runner Power score are game measures. They do not certify fitness or marathon readiness and are not a personalised training plan.

Use your own account and connect only a health account you control. You choose your animal during setup; that choice stays with your runner. Goals and community visibility can be edited in Runner settings.

Imported activity data depends on your device and Google Health. Syncing may be delayed or require renewed permission. Review suspected duplicates before counting them as separate runs.

You can disconnect Google Health or delete your Road to 42 account in the app. See the privacy notice for how your information is handled.
